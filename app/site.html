How it works

How to scan a book to PDF in three steps

No flatbed scanner, no cables and no account.

  1. Hold the phone over the book

    Lean it on a stand or a stack of books. BookSnap outlines the pages live, and you can lock the page area once.

  2. Turn the pages

    In Auto mode the photo is taken a moment after each page turn. Blurry pages and repeats are caught for you.

  3. Save your PDF

    Pages are cleaned like a real scanner and put in order. Save one PDF, split very long books into parts, or share.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is BookSnap free?

Yes. BookSnap is free, with no ads, no account and no page limit.

Can I scan a whole book into one PDF?

Yes. In Auto mode BookSnap takes a photo after every page turn, splits two-page photos into left and right pages, and builds one PDF, even for books with hundreds of pages. Very long books can be split into several PDFs.

How do I scan both sides of an ID card on one page?

Open Card scan, put the front inside the frame and tap the button, then do the same with the back. BookSnap places both sides on one A4 page at real size, ready to print or share as PDF or JPG.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Open E-sign PDF, choose any PDF or one of your scans, draw your signature or use a photo of it, and place it exactly where it belongs. You can also add text and the date.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos, pages, PDFs and your signature are processed and stored on your own device. They only leave it when you share a file or send pages to your own computer or second phone. Read the privacy policy.

Does BookSnap work with Bangla books?

Yes. BookSnap scans Bangla and English books, reads Bangla and English page numbers in Auto mode, and watermarks and signature text can be written in Bangla.

Can I control the scanner from my computer?

Yes. Open PC Remote on your computer and scan its QR code with BookSnap. You see the camera live, press Space to take the photo, and pages are saved straight into a folder on the computer.
