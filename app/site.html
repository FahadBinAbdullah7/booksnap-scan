How it works

Three steps to a finished PDF

No scanner, no cables and no account.

Hold the phone over the book

Lean it on a stand or a stack of books. BookSnap outlines the pages live, and you can lock the area once.

Turn the pages

In Auto mode the photo is taken a moment after each page turn. Blurry pages and repeats are caught for you.

Get your PDF

Pages are cleaned like a real scanner and put in order. Save one PDF, split very long books into parts, or share.
