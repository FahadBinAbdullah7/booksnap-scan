Privacy Policy

BookSnap, for the Android app, the web app and PC Remote · Effective date: 27 November 2026

In short: BookSnap has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos, scanned pages, PDFs and signature are processed and stored on your own device. They are never uploaded to us. They only leave your device when you send them yourself: to your own computer or second phone with PC Remote, or when you share or save a file.

1. Who we are

BookSnap ("the app", "we") is a free document and book scanning app for Android and the web, published by the BookSnap developer. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

2. Data the app handles on your device

  • Camera images: photos you take to scan books, documents, cards and passports. They are turned into pages on your device and stored in the app's private storage.
  • Documents you create: scanned pages, PDFs and images. When you save or export them, they are written to your device (on Android, the Documents/BookSnap folder) or downloaded by your browser.
  • PDFs you open: in E-sign PDF and Watermark PDF, the file you choose is read on your device, and the new PDF is created on your device.
  • Your signature: if you draw or photograph a signature, it is saved only on your device so you can reuse it. You can replace it at any time.
  • Settings: such as scan mode, filters and the code of your last connected PC, stored on your device.

Identity cards and passports can contain personal information. BookSnap treats these scans exactly like any other page: they stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

We do not collect, receive or store any of this data on our servers.

4. Optional features that use the internet

  • PC Remote and Receive on another phone. To connect your phone to your computer or to a second phone, the two devices find each other through a free matchmaking service (PeerJS, 0.peerjs.com). It only sees short connection codes and network addresses, never your images. The live camera view and your pages then travel directly between your two devices over an encrypted WebRTC connection. If a direct connection is not possible, the encrypted data may pass through a relay server (PeerJS TURN servers, or Google's STUN service for finding a route); the relay cannot read it. In local Wi-Fi mode (BookSnap PC on your own computer) everything stays inside your network.
  • Update check (Android app installed outside an app store). The app reads a small version file from our website (booksnap-scanner.vercel.app) and may ask GitHub for the latest release, so it can offer an update. No personal data is sent. If you installed BookSnap from Google Play, updates are delivered by Google Play.
  • Website and web app. The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify you, and we use no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

5. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.

6. Sharing

Files are shared only when you choose to, for example with the Share button (which hands the file to an app you pick, such as email or a cloud drive), or by sending pages to your own computer or phone with PC Remote. The app you share with handles the file under its own privacy policy.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your scans stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete a single page, a book, a card or a scan inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage. Files you exported to the Documents folder stay there until you delete them with your file manager. In the web app, clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything it stored.

8. Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security. Connections between your devices use encrypted WebRTC. Website traffic uses HTTPS.

9. Children

BookSnap is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us through the project page: github.com/FahadBinAbdullah7/booksnap-scanner-web/issues.
